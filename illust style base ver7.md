# Style Lock Guide (2D Vector Line Art)

## 1. Positive Prompt (기본 스타일 가이드)

Subject:
Create a precise 2D vector line illustration of a welcoming, pedestrian-centered urban streetscape for a public design and spatial environment research report. Make the pedestrian route and the arrangement of street furniture immediately readable.

Environment:
Depict a contemporary neighborhood promenade with a broad, continuous walking zone paved with rectangular paving slabs. Arrange slender streetlights, benches with backrests and armrests, and evenly spaced shade trees within a dedicated furnishing strip along one edge. Keep the main walking route unobstructed. Include a flush, step-free transition to a small adjoining plaza. Use simplified low-rise building facades to establish the street boundary, with restrained architectural detail and blank storefront windows.

People and Scale:
Include a few small, naturally proportioned pedestrians to communicate human scale: a person walking, an older adult resting on a bench, and a wheelchair user moving along the clear route. Render everyone with the same simplified outline language, minimal facial detail, and natural poses. Keep people secondary to the spatial design.

Composition:
Use a wide horizontal composition with a gently elevated three-quarter view and restrained two-point perspective. Clearly distinguish the foreground paving, middle-ground furniture and pedestrians, and background facades. Keep vertical lines vertical and spatial relationships coherent. Provide generous breathing room around objects and a quiet upper background. Target a 16:9 landscape layout.

Style:
Clean 2D vector line art with smooth, deliberate paths, rounded line caps, and rounded joins. Use a consistent three-level stroke hierarchy: medium-weight primary contours, lighter internal details, and the finest lines for paving joints and distant architecture. Favor open, unfilled shapes and sparse interior marks. Simplify foliage into a few clear contours. Convey warmth through gently rounded forms and warm line color. Keep every object crisp and legible.

Color Palette:
Use warm ivory (#FAF7F0) for the background and dark warm charcoal (#49433D) for the main outlines. Apply muted sage green (#A8B5A0) and pale sand (#E4D5BF) as small, flat accents on selected foliage and bench surfaces. Keep most surfaces unfilled. Maintain strong outline-to-background contrast and communicate spatial distinctions through shape and line weight as well as color.

Lighting:
Suggest calm, diffuse daylight through the light background and clear outlines. Keep surfaces flat and evenly colored, without modeled lighting, gradients, reflections, or cast shadows.

Visual Consistency and Output:
Maintain one coherent drawing language across buildings, furniture, vegetation, and people. Repeat the same streetlight and bench designs throughout. Convey clarity, care, inclusiveness, and everyday civic comfort. Deliver a clean, presentation-ready illustration with controlled detail, suitable for a research report or public-space concept board. Keep the artwork free of text, labels, logos, dimensions, and decorative borders.
